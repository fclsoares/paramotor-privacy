Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address
  • Name
  • Profile photo (when authorized)

Account and Authentication Data

Zephy allows You to create an account and sign in using third-party identity providers:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Apple Sign-In
  • Facebook Login (Meta)

Where a feature does not require an account, You may use Zephy without signing in. Features that rely on cloud synchronization or a personal profile require authentication.

During authentication, and subject to the permissions You grant, We may receive Your name, email address and profile photo (when authorized). With Apple Sign-In, You may choose to hide Your email address, in which case We receive a private relay address instead. This information is used exclusively for authentication, account management and personalization of Your experience. It is never used for advertising and is never published to any social network.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that You visit, the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

When You access the Service by or through a mobile device, We may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device You use, Your mobile device's unique ID, the IP address of Your mobile device, Your mobile operating system, the type of mobile Internet browser You use, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

We may also collect information that Your browser sends whenever You visit Our Service or when You access the Service by or through a mobile device.

Location Information

Zephy requires access to your device's location in order to provide its core functionality.

When you grant permission, Zephy may access your precise or approximate location while the application is in use.

Your location is used exclusively to:

  • Display your current position on the map;
  • Find nearby flying sites;
  • Retrieve localized weather conditions;
  • Calculate flight recommendations and safe flying windows;
  • Record and display flight tracks;
  • Improve navigation and flight-related features.

Zephy does not sell, rent, or share your location with third parties for advertising purposes.

Location data is only accessed after you explicitly grant permission and only while required for the application's features.

Flight Data

To provide its core flight-logging and instrument features, Zephy records data from Your device's GPS and motion sensors, including:

  • GPS position (latitude and longitude);
  • Altitude and height above ground level (AGL);
  • Speed and direction/heading;
  • Date, time, flight duration and distance traveled;
  • Tracklog (the recorded path of Your flight);
  • Data used to render the 3D Replay.

Flight Data is used to build Your flight history, generate the 3D Replay, calculate flight statistics, enable sharing that You explicitly initiate, and power the instrument, navigation and safety features of the Application. Flight Data is stored on Your device and, when You enable cloud sync or backup, is also stored in the cloud so You can restore it or access it across devices.

Weather Data

Zephy queries third-party meteorological services to provide localized weather information, such as wind speed and direction, temperature, atmospheric pressure and general atmospheric conditions. To retrieve relevant conditions, Your approximate or precise location may be sent to the weather provider. This data is used only to improve Your experience — for example, to display current conditions, recommend safe flying windows and support flight planning — and is not used for advertising.

Why We Collect This Data

We use collected information to:

  • Provide flight planning features;
  • Display weather forecasts;
  • Recommend flying windows;
  • Improve application performance;
  • Fix bugs;
  • Synchronize user information across devices;
  • Enhance user experience.
